## 2. Findings

1. **Compaction does not reduce the prompt, and can grow it (major, APP + MODEL).** Three compactions: 589 → 438 tokens, 1.4k → 539 tokens, 93 → 656 tokens (the app's own numbers on `/desk/0`). Summary length 1,763 → 2,166 → 2,632 chars. After compaction 3 the prompt was 79% of the window, above the 75% trigger, so the next turn compresses again. Each compress call took 209 to 270 s on this model, so a person waits 4 minutes per turn for nothing. Reproduce: window 8192, send ~6 messages of 1.8k chars. APP part: no size target is enforced on the summary (no cap, no "must be at most N% of what it replaces", no check that the prompt is under the threshold afterwards, no skip when the gain is negative); the previous summary is re-summarised together with only one new 93-token message. MODEL part: it writes a narrative that repeats every fact twice (once as fact, once as "the assistant persisted...") and adds a filler recap.
2. **When the fixed part plus the newest message is over the window, the app stops compressing altogether (major, APP).** On d2 the translator ran at 192% and then 276% of its window with ~7k tokens of old history and no compaction; the page only says "only the history is ever compressed, so the prompt is sent as it is". Old history was compressible and was not. With a model server that enforces the window this would be a hard failure; here the server took it (UNVERIFIED what the app shows if the server rejects the prompt: the local server never did).
3. **The fixed prompt is 44% of an 8k window before a word is said (major, APP).** Empty-conversation prompt: 13,231 chars, counted by the app as about 3.6k tokens. A child from the `developer` template starts at 104% of a 4096 window. With a 75% trigger this leaves about 2.5k tokens of history on 8192, and the summary alone takes 440 to 660 of them.
4. **No "refocus": no structured keep-list, no manual trigger, no edit (major against intent, APP).** The summary is free prose. The five elements survived here only because the model chose to keep them; the app has no fixed slots (plan / directions / done / to do / notes) that I could see, and the compaction instruction is not shown anywhere (not in `server.log`, `/events`, spans or the store; spans only give `purpose: compress` and token counts). Threshold is 75%, not the ~90% the owner described.
5. **Standing directions are not durable (major, APP design; minor MODEL slip).** "At most 3 sentences / codename / never Docker / no child engines" live only in the summary prose. They held through 3 compactions, but nothing guarantees it, and the 3-sentence rule was broken once right after compaction 1 (5 sentences). A place for standing directions in the fixed part of the prompt would make this independent of the summariser.
6. **Notes work only by the model's own invention (minor, APP).** "Note this down" led the model to `write(name="blueheron-notes")`, which is durable and shown in every prompt. Good outcome, but nothing in the translator's prompt says so ("plan... is the one artifact you write"), so another run may not do it. The person is not told where the note went unless the model says it.
7. **The person's plan never reaches the `plan` artifact (minor, APP).** The prompt reserves `plan` for child deliverables, so a plan the person dictates stays in chat history and depends on the summary.
8. **Summary 3 ends mid-word: "...without use…" (minor, APP or MODEL, UNVERIFIED which).** Looks like a length cut. A cut at the end of a summary drops the "what to do next" part first, which is the part the owner cares most about.
9. **Feedback during compaction is thin (minor, APP).** Status and page title do say "compressing the history", which is good. But there is no reason given in the chat ("history is at 76% of the window"), no elapsed time, and after it only a collapsed "earlier steps, summarised" block; the before/after token line is far down the engine page.
10. **Leftover `### tools` line after the summary (polish, APP).** After compaction 1 the kept history starts with a `[Tool] write(...) → saved blueheron-notes` result whose call was summarised away (`d/prompts/c1-after.txt` line 111).
11. **A turn at 76% did not compress (polish, APP, UNVERIFIED rule).** Quiz 1 was sent at 6,223 tokens (76%) with no compaction; the next turn compressed. The exact rule (which messages count as "newest") is not explained on the page.

## 3. Usage feedback

Good: the status label "compressing the history"; the engine page line "prompt ≈ 6.2k of 8.2k tokens (76%)" with window, trigger and "compressed N messages (≈ X tokens) into a summary of ≈ Y tokens"; the honest sentence when nothing can be compressed; the full original messages stay in `messages.md` next to the `### summary` blocks; after three compactions the engine still knew the plan, the facts, the open work and the note, and obeyed "no Docker" and "3 sentences".

Confusing or missing: why a turn suddenly takes 4 more minutes; that the compaction saved nothing; no way to say "compress now" or "keep this"; no view of what the summariser was asked to keep; "refocus" does not exist under that or any name; on the small window nothing in the chat warns that the prompt is at 276%.

Five improvements:
1. Make the compaction a structured refocus: fixed headings (plan, standing directions, done, to do, notes) with a size cap, written to an artifact that the person can open and edit.
2. Check the result: if the summary is not clearly smaller than what it replaces, or the prompt is still over the trigger, compress more messages or skip; never re-summarise a summary for one small message.
3. Keep standing directions and noted items outside the history (a `directions`/`notes` artifact the translator is told to use), so they do not depend on the summariser.
4. When the fixed part is over the window, still compress the history, and tell the person in the chat, with the numbers.
5. Show a one-line chat notice at compaction ("history at 76% of 8.2k: summarised 4 messages, 589 → 438 tokens, 3m 46s") and offer a manual "refocus now" control.
