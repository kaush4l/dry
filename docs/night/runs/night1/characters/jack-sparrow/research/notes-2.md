# Notes round 2: films 4 and 5 (On Stranger Tides, Dead Men Tell No Tales)

All pages read 2026-11-23. Paraphrased, no script lines copied. Search engines were blocked this round (DuckDuckGo 403), so these facts come only from the two Wikipedia film articles.

## 2. Creation, actor, inspirations, intent (additions)
- For film 4, Depp worked with the writers on the story design and met them often to show what he wanted to do (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirates_of_the_Caribbean%3A_On_Stranger_Tides, read 2026-11-23)
- With the new director, Rob Marshall, the tone shifted slightly toward a keener eye on history. Rossio thought this fit the franchise (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirates_of_the_Caribbean%3A_On_Stranger_Tides, read 2026-11-23)
- For film 5, Rossio wrote the first script with Depp involved in the writing. Jeff Nathanson wrote a new script in 2013, and the filmmakers took film 1 as their model for script and tone (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirates_of_the_Caribbean%3A_Dead_Men_Tell_No_Tales, read 2026-11-23)
- Film 5 presents him as down on his luck, hunted by the ghost Salazar (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirates_of_the_Caribbean%3A_Dead_Men_Tell_No_Tales, read 2026-11-23)

## 7. Critics on films 4 and 5
- Richard Roeper called film 4 the most fun since the first and said the franchise was tiring but Penélope Cruz energised it (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirates_of_the_Caribbean%3A_On_Stranger_Tides, read 2026-11-23)
- Ann Hornaday (Washington Post) found film 4 as fresh as Jack's first outing and praised Depp. disagrees with Andrew Barker below (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirates_of_the_Caribbean%3A_On_Stranger_Tides, read 2026-11-23)
- Andrew Barker (Variety) felt Geoffrey Rush's Barbossa got the funniest lines and began draining away the roguish charm that had been Depp's trademark. disagrees with Hornaday above (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirates_of_the_Caribbean%3A_On_Stranger_Tides, read 2026-11-23)
- Ray Bennett (Hollywood Reporter) noted lively sexual tension between Cruz's Angelica and Depp's Jack (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirates_of_the_Caribbean%3A_On_Stranger_Tides, read 2026-11-23)
- Mick LaSalle (San Francisco Chronicle) called film 5 a jumble pushed into a played-out franchise (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirates_of_the_Caribbean%3A_Dead_Men_Tell_No_Tales, read 2026-11-23)
- Roeper gave film 5 three stars and liked its steady stream of mild, funny sexual innuendo in the dialogue (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirates_of_the_Caribbean%3A_Dead_Men_Tell_No_Tales, read 2026-11-23)
- Pete Hammond (Deadline) credited Depp with keeping the film and the franchise going. Ashley Esqueda (CNET) called his performance as delightful as ever. disagrees with the Razzie line below (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirates_of_the_Caribbean%3A_Dead_Men_Tell_No_Tales, read 2026-11-23)
- Film 5 got Golden Raspberry nominations for Worst Actor (Depp) and for a Worst Screen Combo of Depp and what it called his worn-out drunk routine. disagrees with Hammond and Esqueda above (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirates_of_the_Caribbean%3A_Dead_Men_Tell_No_Tales, read 2026-11-23)
- Leah Greenblatt (Entertainment Weekly) wished film 5 had taken more risks than repeating the old formula (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirates_of_the_Caribbean%3A_Dead_Men_Tell_No_Tales, read 2026-11-23)

- The A.V. Club described Depp's Jack as louche and campy, and said that in the first three films he was the second banana to the main plot (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirates_of_the_Caribbean%3A_Dead_Men_Tell_No_Tales, read 2026-11-23)
- Analysts put film 5's weak US opening down partly to negative reviews and franchise fatigue (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirates_of_the_Caribbean%3A_Dead_Men_Tell_No_Tales, read 2026-11-23)
- Rossio said his discarded film-5 script, built around a female villain called the Sea Widow, was dropped mainly because Depp worried it would repeat Dark Shadows. This shows Depp steering Jack's stories (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirates_of_the_Caribbean%3A_Dead_Men_Tell_No_Tales, read 2026-11-23)
- unconfirmed: Tasha Robinson reviewed film 4 for The A.V. Club, but her view of Jack was not in the lines read (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirates_of_the_Caribbean%3A_On_Stranger_Tides, read 2026-11-23)
