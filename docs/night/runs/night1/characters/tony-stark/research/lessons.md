# Lessons from this round

1. Search budget is small. DuckDuckGo went to 403 and Wikipedia to 429 after about ten calls. Next time fetch the key film pages first (Endgame, Iron Man 3, Civil War, Homecoming), before any open search.
2. When every source refuses, stop; do not work around the block. Reading results already listed from other sites still worked.
3. focus() on a long file did not return the text to me. Printing chunks of at most 15 lines with sed did. Use sed for long sources.
4. One act failed to parse because of a stray tag, and its four calls had to be redone. Keep each act plain calls only.
5. A turn ran past 600s with no reply, so nothing reached the user. Write the output files early, and in a single step.
6. Two Wikipedia pages disagree on whether the 2008 script was finished. Record both and do not pick one.
7. Secondhand reports (IndieWire for Esquire, CBR for Vanity Fair, Fiction Horizon for People) should be marked as secondhand.
8. The gaps (FRIDAY, drinking, Howard, Rhodey, tech jargon, deflection) need the Marvel Fandom wiki or Downey interviews. Try those first next round.
9. Nickname lists are many but similar. Two named critics plus Collider were enough; more lists add nothing.
