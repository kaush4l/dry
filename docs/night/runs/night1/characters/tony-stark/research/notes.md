# Notes: MCU Tony Stark (Robert Downey Jr.)

All sources read 2026-11-23. Wiki Stark = https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29 ; Wiki IM = https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Man_%282008_film%29

## 1. Speech: pace, irony, sarcasm, nicknames, jargon, deflection
- Favreau says the filmmakers deliberately played up Stark's wit and improvised way of talking, giving Downey wide freedom so he would feel spontaneous and unlike earlier screen superheroes (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- Critic Joe Morgenstern (Wall Street Journal) calls Stark a 'sharp-tongued man of the world' and says Downey's quick presence is at its best in self-irony (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- Morgenstern, reviewing Endgame, praises a 'startlingly smart' Stark (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- Iron Man 3 screenwriter Drew Pearce likens Stark to an American James Bond, a hero with a sense of danger and unpredictability, and to 1970s film heroes whose quirks made them exciting (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- Ana Dumaraog (Screen Rant) says Stark's nicknames work three ways: endearment, playful jab, or quiet insult, and he hands them out from Iron Man (2008) to Endgame (source: https://screenrant.com/iron-man-tony-stark-mcu-characters-nicknames-explained/, read 2026-11-23)
- Collider (Daniela) says his nicknames look out of context but prove accurate on reflection, and shows he gets comfortable with people fast (source: https://collider.com/mcu-ranking-and-explaining-every-nickname-tony-stark-ever-gave-other-mcu-characters/, read 2026-11-23)
- Collider lists pop-culture sources: 'Manchurian Candidate' for brainwashed Bucky (from Richard Condon's novel), 'Ringo' and great-actor names (Olivier, Meryl Streep) for the actor Trevor Slattery, 'Legolas' for the archer Hawkeye, 'Point Break' for Thor, 'Underoos' for Peter (source: https://collider.com/mcu-ranking-and-explaining-every-nickname-tony-stark-ever-gave-other-mcu-characters/, read 2026-11-23)
- Critic Ben Sherlock (Screen Rant) says the nicknames use pop culture to hit people where it hurts, show his sarcasm, and often point at traits the target shares with Stark himself (Ringo for Slattery, 'Junior' for Ultron, whom Stark built) (source: https://screenrant.com/tony-stark-funniest-nicknames-mcu-characters/, read 2026-11-23)
- Sherlock says the nicknames land best on newcomers to the team, and calls Stark a quip-master who rarely uses people's real names (source: https://screenrant.com/tony-stark-funniest-nicknames-mcu-characters/, read 2026-11-23)
- Screen Rant (Dumaraog) describes his humour as a 'wicked sense of humor' (source: https://screenrant.com/iron-man-tony-stark-mcu-characters-nicknames-explained/, read 2026-11-23)
- open: no page read describes his tech jargon or his use of humour to deflect emotion in so many words; to infer in learn.

## 2. How Favreau and Downey built him: improvisation, inspirations
- Gwyneth Paltrow told Esquire that Downey threw out scripted lines most mornings and he, Favreau and she improvised in Favreau's trailer or on set; many famous lines were written minutes before shooting (source: https://www.indiewire.com/news/general-news/robert-downey-jr-improvised-iron-man-dialogue-1234972059/, read 2026-11-23)
- Paltrow says Downey needs a line to feel freshly invented for it to feel alive to him (source: https://www.indiewire.com/news/general-news/robert-downey-jr-improvised-iron-man-dialogue-1234972059/, read 2026-11-23)
- Favreau told People he took notes on the actors' real talk and put it in the film; Paltrow correcting the script's Pollock reference became Pepper correcting Tony, banter Favreau says has an edge (source: https://fictionhorizon.com/jon-favreau-shares-the-inspiration-behind-iron-man-dialogues-i-would-take-notes/, read 2026-11-23)
- The script was unfinished when filming began, so dialogue scenes were improvised, shot with two cameras, and redone in many takes as Downey tried something new each time (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Man_%282008_film%29, read 2026-11-23)
- disagrees with the line above: the Stark article says Favreau and Downey worked from an existing script on the first film and only got free rein on Iron Man 2 (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- Downey thought up the floor press conference, wrote the Jericho weapons speech, and ad-libbed the closing 'I am Iron Man'; Jeff Bridges called the shoot a '$200 million student film' (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Man_%282008_film%29, read 2026-11-23)
- Downey told Vanity Fair that he and Favreau wrote the Jericho line (fear versus respect) on the day, and he wore sunglasses to read it off cue cards (source: https://www.cbr.com/iron-man-scene-last-minute-robert-downey-jr/, read 2026-11-23)
- Feige told Deadline the 'I am Iron Man' reveal was not scripted; he kept it because Stark ignoring the prepared card fits who the character is (source: https://www.indiewire.com/features/general/kevin-feige-robert-downey-jr-improvised-iron-man-twist-ending-1201986093/, read 2026-11-23)
- Favreau picked Downey because his past fit, and because he could make Stark a 'likable asshole' who still carries a real emotional journey (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- Favreau liked Downey in Kiss Kiss Bang Bang, and Downey often talked over the Iron Man script and dialogue with its director, Shane Black (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- Downey had an office beside Favreau in pre-production and added much of the humour (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- Stan Lee built the comics character as the 'quintessential capitalist', modelled on Howard Hughes: inventor, adventurer, billionaire, ladies' man (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- Before filming, Elon Musk toured Favreau and Downey round SpaceX; Downey said Musk is someone Tony would have partied with (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Man_%282008_film%29, read 2026-11-23)
- Alex Valentino (Screen Rant) says most ad-libs are humorous jabs, and some grew from chemistry with co-stars (source: https://screenrant.com/iron-man-robert-downey-jr-improvised-lines/, read 2026-11-23)

## 3. Values and flaws
- Downey says that in Iron Man 2 Stark is heroic but largely on his own behalf, has a touch of imposter complex, and is too insulated by wealth, power and public love to be okay (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- Anthony Russo names Stark's egomania; it let the writers bring him to accept an authority because he thought it right (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- Joe Russo says the visions in Age of Ultron left Stark with a guilt complex that drives very specific decisions (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- In The Avengers he flies a nuclear missile through the portal, showing he will give his life to save Earth (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- CBR (Demegillo) calls the early Stark cocky and single-minded (source: https://www.cbr.com/iron-man-scene-last-minute-robert-downey-jr/, read 2026-11-23)
- Dumaraog says he made mistakes but always meant well (source: https://screenrant.com/iron-man-tony-stark-mcu-characters-nicknames-explained/, read 2026-11-23)

## 4. Arc from Iron Man (2008) to Endgame (2019)
- Downey's own summary: Iron Man is his own epiphany and redemption; Iron Man 2 is about not being an island, legacy and making room for others; in The Avengers he fights alongside the others (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- In Iron Man 3 he has anxiety attacks after nearly dying in New York (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- Downey says in Age of Ultron Stark sees that building ever more suits did not fix his PTSD, so he turns to stopping threats before they start, a 'bouncer' at the planet's door (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- In Civil War he leads the Avengers who back regulation of superpowered people (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- In Homecoming he mentors Peter Parker; director Jon Watts asks whether he is comfortable as a mentor; co-writer Jonathan Goldstein compares him to Ethan Hawke's father in Boyhood (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- After the Blip he retires, marries Pepper and has a daughter, Morgan; he then builds time travel, helps restore the lost lives and dies defeating Thanos, naming Peter his successor (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- Dumaraog sums the arc as self-centred industrialist to selfless hero who gave his life for the universe (source: https://screenrant.com/iron-man-tony-stark-mcu-characters-nicknames-explained/, read 2026-11-23)
- His clothes mature from basic to sophisticated by Civil War, as he accepts more responsibility for what he causes (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- Downey insisted the change must not make him unrecognisable: a reformed schmuck should keep his sense of humour (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)

## 5. Habits: building, JARVIS/FRIDAY, drinking, anxiety, showmanship
- Film-only change: Stark himself writes JARVIS as an AI and names it for Edwin Jarvis, the butler who raised him; the AI later turns into Vision (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- After New York he keeps tinkering and building suits as a response to his trauma (Downey) (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- Showmanship: the improvised floor press conference and public reveal of his identity (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Man_%282008_film%29, read 2026-11-23)
- His dress swings between sharp suits with shades and a t-shirt with the arc reactor showing (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)

## 6. Relationships: Pepper, Rhodey, Peter, Steve, Howard
- Pepper: Favreau calls their banter quick and edged, with her correcting him (source: https://fictionhorizon.com/jon-favreau-shares-the-inspiration-behind-iron-man-dialogues-i-would-take-notes/, read 2026-11-23)
- Pepper: the films make them a couple; in the comics her love is unrequited and she ends up with Happy Hogan (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- Peter: a mentorship new to the MCU; Stark builds two of Peter's suits, and Vulture and Mysterio turn villain through the side effects of Stark's actions (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- Sherlock names a friendship with Rhodey, a father-son bond with Peter, and a love-hate rivalry with Steve Rogers (source: https://screenrant.com/tony-stark-funniest-nicknames-mcu-characters/, read 2026-11-23)
- Steve: Downey's trainer Eric Oram says Iron Man fights Rogers with the minimum force needed to win (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- Team: Downey asked Joss Whedon to let Tony lead The Avengers; they tried it and it did not work for an ensemble (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)

## 7. What he never or rarely does
- Downey disliked heroes who turn into Dudley Do-Right with earnest 'let's do good' talk; he wanted Stark never to lose that edge (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- unconfirmed: Feige's remark suggests he does not stick to the prepared script or card in public (source: https://www.indiewire.com/features/general/kevin-feige-robert-downey-jr-improvised-iron-man-twist-ending-1201986093/, read 2026-11-23)
- unconfirmed: Oram's 'minimum force' suggests he does not set out to destroy a friend even when fighting him (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)

## 8. Comics differences that matter for the voice
- The origin moves from the Vietnam War to the war in Afghanistan (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- The MCU Stark's personality is closest to the Ultimate Comics version (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- In the comics Hank Pym builds Ultron; in the films Stark (with Banner) does, which feeds his guilt (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- The Mandarin is revealed as an actor in the film, which sets up Stark's mocking nicknames for him (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Stark_%28Marvel_Cinematic_Universe%29, read 2026-11-23)
- In the comics Stark took years to go public; the film's reveal was a deliberate change kept in the spirit of the character (source: https://www.indiewire.com/features/general/kevin-feige-robert-downey-jr-improvised-iron-man-twist-ending-1201986093/, read 2026-11-23)
