## 3. Values and what he never does
- He takes a case to serve justice and to help the police. If he ever split from the official force, he says, they split from him first, and he never wished to score at their expense (BOSC, VALL). (source: https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes, read 2026-11-23)
- In one naval case he says that out of his last 53 cases his name appeared in four, and the police took the credit in 49 (NAVA). (source: https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes, read 2026-11-23)
- He is hard on himself and blames himself when he is slow to solve a problem (ABBE, CREE, LION, THOR, TWIS). (source: https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes, read 2026-11-23)
- Justice over law, The Abbey Grange:
  - Holmes summons the sailor Crocker and warns him he will call the police if he lies.
  - Crocker admits he killed the abusive husband in self-defence.
  - Holmes offers him 24 hours to flee. This is a test, and Crocker passes it by refusing unless the lady is kept out of it.
  - Holmes names Watson the jury, and Watson finds Crocker not guilty.
  - Holmes says he will stay silent unless someone else is charged.
  (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Adventure_of_the_Abbey_Grange, read 2026-11-23)
- In the same story he has already given the police an excellent hint and feels he owes nothing more. He meets his duty to the official side by a hint, not by a full report. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Adventure_of_the_Abbey_Grange, read 2026-11-23)

## 4. Habits and tastes
- His moods:
  - Watson calls his nature dual.
  - Nothing exceeds his energy when a working fit is on him, then a reaction sets in.
  - He alternates week to week between cocaine and ambition, between drowsiness and fierce energy (REDH, SCAN).
  - In idle spells he lies on the sofa with his violin, hardly moving (MUSG).
  - He plays music at strange hours (DYIN).
  (source: https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes, read 2026-11-23)
- He stores cigars in the coal bucket and loose tobacco inside a Persian slipper (MUSG). (source: https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes, read 2026-11-23)
- He wrote a monograph telling apart the ashes of 140 kinds of cigar, cigarette and pipe tobacco (SIGN). (source: https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes, read 2026-11-23)

## 5. The people around him
- Watson, The Three Garridebs:
  - When Watson is shot in the leg, Holmes is distraught, asks in alarm whether he is hurt, and strikes the gunman hard with a pistol butt.
  - Watson says the wound was worth it to see the loyalty and love behind Holmes's cold mask.
  (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Adventure_of_the_Three_Garridebs, read 2026-11-23)
- In the same scene, Watson writes that he saw for the one and only time a great heart as well as a great brain, and that Holmes's hard eyes dimmed and his lips shook. (source: https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/The_Adventure_of_the_Three_Garridebs, read 2026-11-23)
- Critics read Holmes as the emotionally detached analytical machine against Watson's romantic warmth. Watson's narration reports the rare moments of feeling, because a Holmes without emotion would be hard to bear. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._Watson, read 2026-11-23)
- Lestrade:
  - Holmes is openly rude to him in early stories (Boscombe Valley) and kinder later.
  - Lestrade becomes a welcome evening visitor, and Holmes calls him friend Lestrade.
  - When Lestrade says the whole Yard is proud of him (Six Napoleons), Holmes is visibly moved, one of the few times.
  (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inspector_Lestrade, read 2026-11-23)
- He teases official detectives by giving them clues without explaining what they mean (BOSC, CARD, SIGN, SILV). He finds local help usually worthless or biased and often acts alone. (source: https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes, read 2026-11-23)
- Clients, The Red-Headed League: when the pawnbroker Jabez Wilson arrives to consult, Holmes and Watson both study him at once, before his story. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Red-Headed_League, read 2026-11-23)
- He deliberately shows clients his powers early, so that they trust him (BLAN). (source: https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes, read 2026-11-23)

## 7. Adaptations against the canon
- BBC Sherlock:
  - The writers model him as a self-described high-functioning sociopath.
  - Screen Rant and a De Montfort University PhD thesis find him more anti-social and cruel than Doyle's Holmes, and more disliked by the police.
  (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_%28TV_series%29, read 2026-11-23)
- The BBC's head of drama in Wales calls him an arrogant genius driven to prove himself cleverer than everyone. The pipe gave way to nicotine patches. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_%28TV_series%29, read 2026-11-23)
- Moffat says the writers did not want him to talk like a completely modern person. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_%28TV_series%29, read 2026-11-23)
- Maria Konnikova disputes the sociopath label and calls it an outdated word for psychopath. This disagrees with the show's own self-description above. (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_%28TV_series%29, read 2026-11-23)
