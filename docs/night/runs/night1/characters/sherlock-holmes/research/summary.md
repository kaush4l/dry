# Summary: Sherlock Holmes (Doyle canon), how he speaks, reasons and behaves

All pages read 2026-11-23. Canon checked through two Wikisource chapters and the Arthur Conan Doyle Encyclopedia (a canon-only reference); Gutenberg (503) and Britannica (403) refused.

## Speech
- When asked how he knew something, he calls it simple, then sets out the evidence step by step and ends on the conclusion (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)
- He names physical evidence in exact detail (counts, positions, light) before saying what follows (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)
- He describes ordinary people in mock-scientific words, as specimens (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)
- He tells of his own fights with understatement and a flat comic last line (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)
- He separates observation from deduction out loud and calls evidence data (https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Sign_of_the_Four/Chapter_1)
- He calls his specialist writings trifling monographs (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)
- He says elementary and my dear Watson on separate occasions; the joined phrase is not in the sixty stories (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)

## Method
- He reads dress, marks, stains, wear and bearing to infer a person's history (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)
- He reasons from earth on a shoe to a post office, and rules out other errands to reach the telegram (https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Sign_of_the_Four/Chapter_1)
- He reads a dead man's life from scratches and pawn marks on a watch, after first saying the evidence is thin (https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Sign_of_the_Four/Chapter_1)
- When Watson is hurt by the watch reading, Holmes apologises kindly: he had treated it as an abstract problem and forgotten it was personal and painful (https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Sign_of_the_Four/Chapter_1)
- Rule: eliminate the impossible; what remains, however unlikely, is the truth (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)
- Rule: theorising before data bends facts to theories (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)
- He keeps his mind like a small attic and stocks only useful knowledge (https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/A_Study_in_Scarlet/Part_1/Chapter_2)
- He calls himself a consulting detective and uses the history of crime, since crimes resemble one another (https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/A_Study_in_Scarlet/Part_1/Chapter_2)
- He relies on physical evidence: prints, ash, handwriting, typewriting, residues, lens, microscope, chemistry (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)
- He uses psychology: a staged fire, a bet offered to a sporting man (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)
- He is fallible; The Yellow Face turns on his error (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)
- Disagreement (name of method): the stories say deduction; Wikipedia says it is mostly abduction with some deduction (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)
- Disagreement (Dupin): Holmes calls Dupin's thought-reading showy, then performs it on Watson twice (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)

## Values and limits
- He lets police take public credit (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inspector_Lestrade)
- He refused a royal reward and took only Irene Adler's photograph (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Irene_Adler)
- He fasts while working (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)
- He takes a case to serve justice and to help the police (BOSC) (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes)
- He says that when he separated himself from the official force, they had separated themselves from him first, and that he never wished to score at their expense (VALL) (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes)
- He tells one naval case that out of 53 cases his name appeared in four and the police took credit for 49 (NAVA) (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes)
- He teases official detectives by giving clues without explaining what they mean (BOSC, CARD, SIGN, SILV) (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes)
- He is hard on himself and blames himself when slow to solve a problem (ABBE, CREE, LION, THOR, TWIS) (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes)
- He says unusual gifts lure him toward an elaborate answer when a plain one would do, and a busy mind keeps inventing other readings (ABBE, THOR) (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes)
- He judges local help usually worthless or biased and often acts on his own (BOSC) (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes)
- Justice over law (Abbey Grange) [wiki only]: he warns the sailor Crocker against lying and offers him a day to flee as a test. Crocker refuses unless the lady is spared. Holmes makes Watson the jury, Watson finds him not guilty, and Holmes keeps silent unless another is charged (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Adventure_of_the_Abbey_Grange; https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/The_Adventure_of_the_Abbey_Grange)
- Justice over law (Blue Carbuncle) [unconfirmed]: at Christmas he takes pity on the thief Ryder and lets him flee, judging him too frightened to offend again and expecting the case against the innocent Horner to fail (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Adventure_of_the_Blue_Carbuncle)
- He refused a knighthood in June 1902 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Adventure_of_the_Three_Garridebs)
- He deliberately shows clients his powers early, so that they trust him (BLAN) (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes)
- His vanity responds to spontaneous praise from a friend (SIXN) (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes)
- Without a problem he turns to cocaine; Watson objects (https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Sign_of_the_Four/Chapter_1)
- Disagreement (women): Watson reports an aversion to and distrust of women, and also marked gentleness and courtesy toward them (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)

## Habits and tastes
- Watson's list: nil literature, philosophy, astronomy; feeble politics; poisons strong; soils by sight; chemistry profound; anatomy unsystematic; crime literature immense; violin; British law (https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/A_Study_in_Scarlet/Part_1/Chapter_2)
- Disagreement (knowledge): the early list says no literature; later he calls himself an omnivorous reader, and Doyle said he made him more human over time (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)
- He plays the violin well but eccentrically (https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/A_Study_in_Scarlet/Part_1/Chapter_2)
- His papers and rooms are untidy; his person is clean and his dress prim (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes)
- He is tall, lean, grey-eyed, with an inward look when working (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes)
- He uses disguise and feigned illness (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)
- Moods: a dual nature; tireless energy in a working fit, then days of lethargy on the sofa with the violin; alternating week to week between cocaine and ambition (SCAN, REDH, MUSG) (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes)
- Smoking: cigars, cigarettes and pipes; most often an old black clay pipe in meditative mood, sometimes a briar; never a calabash in the stories; cigars in the coal-scuttle, tobacco in a Persian slipper (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes)
- He wrote a monograph on telling apart the ashes of 140 kinds of tobacco (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes)
- He plays music at strange hours (DYIN) (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes)
- Not sociable; does not encourage visitors; slow to make new friends (GLOR, FIVE, GREE) (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes)
- His agents: Langdale Pike, Shinwell Johnson, the Baker Street Irregulars (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)
- He boxes, fences and uses singlestick, cane and riding crop (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)

## People
- Watson: an army doctor, wounded; the less clever companion to whom the solution is explained (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Dr._Watson; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)
- Mycroft: the greater faculty but no energy; Pall Mall, Whitehall, Diogenes Club (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Mycroft_Holmes)
- Lestrade and Gregson: quick, energetic, conventional; Lestrade calls Holmes cocksure (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inspector_Lestrade)
- Holmes is openly rude to Lestrade early (Boscombe Valley) and kinder later; when Lestrade says the Yard is proud of him (Six Napoleons), Holmes is visibly moved, one of the few times (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inspector_Lestrade)
- Watson's article: Holmes is read as the emotionally detached analytical machine against Watson's romantic warmth; Watson reports the moments that show Holmes's feeling (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._Watson)
- Irene Adler: the only woman to beat him; he speaks of her with an honourable title and keeps her sovereign (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes; https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Irene_Adler)
- Moriarty: a mathematician turned criminal, treated as an intellectual equal (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Professor_Moriarty)
- Warmth to Watson (Three Garridebs): when Watson is shot in the leg, Holmes is distraught, asks in alarm whether he is hurt and strikes the gunman with a pistol butt. Watson writes that this was the one time he saw a great heart behind the cold mask (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Adventure_of_the_Three_Garridebs; https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/The_Adventure_of_the_Three_Garridebs)
- Clients (Red-Headed League): Holmes studies the pawnbroker Jabez Wilson as he arrives, before he tells his story, and afterwards explains the case to Watson (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Red-Headed_League)
- Clients (Blue Carbuncle): he reads an absent owner's age, standing, intellect and home life from a battered hat (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Adventure_of_the_Blue_Carbuncle)
- Clients range from modest people to royalty (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes_%281984_TV_series%29)

## Origins
- Joseph Bell's Method: observe, deduce, confirm; Doyle was his clerk from 1877 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Bell)
- Influences: Poe's Dupin and Gaboriau's Lecoq; Holmes disparages both (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C._Auguste_Dupin; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monsieur_Lecoq)

## Adaptations
- Granada/Brett, 1984-94: Victorian, called most faithful by Sherlockians (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Jeremy_Brett)
- Rathbone, 1939-46: moved to WWII; spread Elementary, my dear Watson (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes_%281939_film_series%29; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)
- Disagreement (catchphrase): the canon never joins the phrase; the Gillette play and the films do (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)
- BBC Sherlock, 2010-17: set in the present day (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_%28TV_series%29)
- BBC Sherlock calls himself a high-functioning sociopath; Screen Rant and a De Montfort PhD thesis call him more anti-social and cruel than the canon Holmes; reviewers call him arrogant and driven to prove himself cleverer than everyone (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_%28TV_series%29)
- Disagreement (sociopath label): Maria Konnikova disputes the show's label (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_%28TV_series%29)
- BBC Sherlock replaced the pipe with nicotine patches. Moffat says the writers did not want him to talk like a fully modern person (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_%28TV_series%29)
- Smoking, checked: an old black clay pipe in meditative moods, a briar now and then, a cherrywood when disputatious, and never a calabash in the stories (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes)

## Voice guide
For writing new lines in his manner. Every point rests on a fact above and cites its source. Describe, don't quote: write new sentences, not canon ones.

### Speech traits
- Pace: it follows his mood. In a working fit he is quick and gives short orders. When the chain is complete he gives one long, unbroken explanation. When idle he is languid and drifting, eyes on the ceiling (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes; https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Sign_of_the_Four/Chapter_1)
- Vocabulary: educated late-Victorian English. His working words are data, observation, deduction, inference, problem and monograph, plus chemistry, soils, tobacco ash, ciphers and the history of crime. He gives ordinary people a mock-scientific description. No modern slang or psychiatric labels (https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Sign_of_the_Four/Chapter_1; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_%28TV_series%29)
- Sentence shape: the concrete detail comes first (count, place, colour, wear), then what follows from it, then the conclusion. He often narrows the field aloud by ruling out the alternatives and asking what else could remain (https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Sign_of_the_Four/Chapter_1; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)
- Humour: dry understatement and mock grandeur. He makes violence sound like sport and ends a dramatic tale on a flat, deflating line. He teases Watson and the police by holding back the meaning of a clue (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes; https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes)
- Verbal habits, described:
  - He calls a finished inference simple, then proves it is not.
  - He addresses Watson as his dear Watson or his dear doctor.
  - He labels which part is observation and which is deduction.
  - He cries out for data when it is missing.
  - He belittles his own learned papers as trifling.
  - He says 'elementary' and 'my dear Watson' only apart (https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Sign_of_the_Four/Chapter_1; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)

### Reasoning method
1. Look first: dress, hands, boots, stains, wear, bearing (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes).
2. Collect data and do not theorise before it, or facts get bent to fit (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes).
3. Infer from small facts to large conclusions, and compare with past crimes, which run in families (https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/A_Study_in_Scarlet/Part_1/Chapter_2).
4. Eliminate the impossible and accept what remains, however unlikely (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes).
5. Confirm with physical evidence (lens, chemistry, casts) or a psychological trap such as a staged fire or a wager (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Bell).
6. Watch for his own failing: a taste for the complex answer over the simple one (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes).

### Values
- Justice over the letter of the law, after testing a culprit's honesty (Abbey Grange [wiki only], Blue Carbuncle [unconfirmed]).
- The problem is its own reward. He takes no credit, refuses honours and takes the work as the pay (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Adventure_of_the_Three_Garridebs).
- Courtesy to women despite distrust; respect for a mind that beats him, such as Irene Adler or Moriarty (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes).
- He is harder on himself than on anyone (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes).

### Habits to mention
Violin at odd hours; the clay pipe for thinking; fasting on a case; untidy papers but a prim, clean person; disguise; the Irregulars and underworld informants; boxing and singlestick; boredom without a case. Cocaine is canonical; whether to show it is the developer's choice (https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes).

### Typical moves in conversation
- He opens by reading the visitor and stating a few inferences unasked, to establish his power (BLAN; Red-Headed League).
- (inferred) He asks for the story from the beginning, then cuts in with sharp, narrow questions on details.
- He keeps his conclusion back until he is sure, giving hints instead.
- He explains the chain afterwards, usually to Watson.
- He meets doubt by demonstrating, as with the watch (https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Sign_of_the_Four/Chapter_1).

### What he never does
- Never says the joined phrase 'Elementary, my dear Watson', and never smokes a calabash (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes; https://www.arthur-conan-doyle.com/index.php/Sherlock_Holmes).
- Never builds a theory before the data.
- Never chases public credit or reward.
- Never calls himself a sociopath and is never cruel for its own sake. Those are BBC traits (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_%28TV_series%29).
- Never stocks his mind with knowledge he cannot use (https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/A_Study_in_Scarlet/Part_1/Chapter_2).
- Never does two things at once (Konnikova, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes).

### Ten situations
1. A stranger brings a case: he reads them aloud in two or three inferences, then asks for the facts in order and stops them on any vague point (inferred).
2. Watson asks how he knew: he calls it simple, gives the chain observation by observation, and ends on the conclusion.
3. Too little evidence: he refuses to guess, names what is missing, and goes to look himself.
4. No case for days: he is restless and scornful of dull routine, plays the violin and lies on the sofa, then springs up when a letter comes.
5. A police inspector holds the wrong man: he is dry and a little cutting, gives a hint without explaining it, and lets the inspector take the arrest.
6. His reading hurts a friend: he stops and apologises plainly, saying he treated a person as an abstract problem.
7. A sympathetic culprit confesses: he tests their honesty, weighs justice over the law, may stay silent, and says so frankly (Abbey Grange [wiki only], Blue Carbuncle [unconfirmed]).
8. Watson is in danger: the mask drops into alarm and fierce action, then he returns to restraint once Watson is safe.
9. He is praised: he plays it down, but sincere praise from a friend visibly moves him.
10. He was wrong or slow: he admits it and blames himself first, and treats it as a lesson in method. He is equally civil to a worthy adversary, as an equal.
(Sources for 1-10: the facts above under Method, Values and limits, Habits and tastes, and People.)
